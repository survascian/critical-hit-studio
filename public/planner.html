SETTINGS

Theme

Changes the look and shape of the Planner (and the top header on the Hit List; the Hit List board itself stays untouched).

Board cards

Choose what shows on each card. Nothing is deleted; hidden items stay in the game's details.

Video formats

Customize the formats you make (label, short card tag, and badge colour).

Ownership labels (optional text under card posters)

Upload schedule

Pick your upload days to mark slots on the calendar, and optionally show the video buffer strip.

Stage checklists

The to-do list each game gets when it enters a stage (one item per line). Existing cards keep their current lists.

Your YouTube channel

Link your handle or channel URL (e.g. @Survascian). Shows your handle next to the version, puts your channel avatar and a one-click YouTube Studio button in the top right.

YouTube Data API key (optional)

Enables your channel avatar, subscriber/view stats, public upload picker, and per-video stats. Get a free key at console.cloud.google.com → enable YouTube Data API v3 → Create credentials → API key. Stored only on this computer.

IGDB connection

Free via Twitch: (1) sign in at dev.twitch.tv/console (requires 2FA), (2) Register Your Application with OAuth Redirect URL http://localhost, Category Application Integration, Client Type Confidential, (3) copy the Client ID and click New Secret.

    IGDB sync

    Re-fetches every IGDB-linked game (genres, themes, platforms, time to beat). Your own title, stage, hours, links and manual genres are kept.

    SteamGridDB (posters, banners & logos)

    Pick custom posters, Hit List banners, and transparent logos from steamgriddb.com. Get a free key at steamgriddb.com/profile/preferences/api.

    Cloud Sync (PC ↔ Phone, optional)

    Sign in with an email & password to automatically sync your planner & journal between your PC and the mobile app in real time. Local backups always stay on this PC.
